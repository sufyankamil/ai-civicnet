Child Safety Standards

Last updated: March 3, 2026

CivicNet is a community help platform that connects neighbours to assist one another with everyday tasks. We are deeply committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

CivicNet has a strict zero-tolerance policy toward any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or child sexual exploitation and abuse (CSAE). Any content, behaviour, or account found to involve the exploitation or abuse of minors will result in immediate account termination and will be reported to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable.

2. Age Requirement

CivicNet is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users must be at least 13 years of age to create an account. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to register or use the platform. If we become aware that a user is under 13, their account will be promptly removed.

3. Content Moderation & Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited on CivicNet:

  • Any imagery, text, video, or link that sexually exploits or abuses minors.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any communication intended to exploit children.
  • Content that normalises or glorifies violence, abuse, or exploitation of minors.
  • Sharing personal information of minors without parental consent.

4. Reporting Mechanisms

Users can report any content or behaviour they believe violates our child safety standards directly within the app. All reports are reviewed promptly. You can also contact us directly at:

📧 civicnet.app@gmail.com
Please include as much detail as possible so we can investigate effectively.

5. Cooperation with Authorities

CivicNet will fully cooperate with law enforcement agencies and regulatory authorities investigating cases of child exploitation or abuse. We will preserve and provide evidence as required by applicable law.

6. Prevention Practices

  • Account verification: Email verification is required to create an account.
  • User reporting: In-app reporting tools allow any user to flag inappropriate content or behaviour.
  • Data security: All user data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored securely using industry-standard practices.
  • Regular reviews: We periodically review our safety practices to ensure they meet current standards.

7. Contact & Designated Point of Contact

For any concerns, questions, or reports related to child safety on CivicNet, please reach out to our designated safety contact:

📧 civicnet.app@gmail.com

8. External Resources

  • NCMEC CyberTipline – Report child exploitation online.
  • Internet Watch Foundation (IWF) – Report CSAM content.
