How a request works

From asking to saying thanks, in four steps.

  1. 1

    Post what you need

    Type it or say it out loud. CivicNet adds a category and shows your request to people close by.

  2. 2

    Neighbors offer to help

    People who can help respond with their time, tools or skills. You choose who to accept.

  3. 3

    Chat opens when you accept

    Private messages unlock only between you and the helper you picked.

  4. 4

    Mark it done and say thanks

    Rate the help. Helpers earn points and karma for every request they complete.

Questions people ask

Still unsure? Write to civicnet.app@gmail.com.

What is CivicNet?

CivicNet connects people who need help with neighbors nearby who have the time, tools or skills to give it.

Is CivicNet free?

Yes. CivicNet is free, with a monthly allowance of AI actions. CivicNet Pro removes that limit and starts with a 7-day free trial.

Who can see my request?

People nearby see the request you post and your public profile (name, photo and skills). Private chat opens only between you and the helper you accept.

How are helpers matched to my request?

By distance, skills and urgency. People close to you see it first, helper profiles are compared with what you asked for, and emergencies are lifted to the top.

How do I cancel CivicNet Pro?

Open your App Store subscription settings and cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the app, or send a request on our delete account page. Your account and data are removed.

Which languages does CivicNet support?

The app is available in English and Hindi.
