Data Deletion Request

Option 1 — Delete directly from the app

1

Open the CivicNet app and go to your Profile.

2

Tap Settings (gear icon in the top right).

3

Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account".

4

Confirm the deletion. Your account and all associated data will be permanently removed within 24 hours.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, send us an email with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your registered email address. We will process your request within 7 business days.

📧 Email Us to Delete Your Data

What gets deleted?

  • Your account and profile information
  • All help requests you posted
  • Your messages and chat history
  • Ratings and points history
  • Push notification tokens
⚠️ Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Anonymised, aggregated statistics (not linked to your identity) may be retained for service improvement.
